🎤 Speaking

Build your own STAR story

Choose a real story from your story bank.

S · Situation
Where and when?
Frame: Last… at my job, …
T · Task
What was your responsibility?
Frame: I had to… / My job was to…
A · Action
What did YOU do? (2–3 steps)
Frame: First, I… Then I… After that, I…
R · Result
What happened? Add a number if you can.
Frame: As a result, we… I learned that…
